# TradeSphere

### Participation Markets for Humans and Autonomous Agents

TradeSphere is an execution-driven platform for building and operating decentralized participation markets.

At its core is a shared environment where humans, autonomous agents, organizations, and activities interact through common rules, resources, incentives, and economic mechanisms.

Rather than treating applications as isolated systems, TradeSphere provides an integrated architecture for coordinating participation across different economic and operational domains.

**One architecture. Multiple domains. Human and machine participation.**

---

## Architecture

The **TradeSphere Engine** brings together a collection of services that provide the execution environment, domain models, and capabilities underlying the platform.

| Service | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Platform** | Runtime environment, timing, ticks, intervals, and test, simulation, and live execution |
| **Sphere** | Organizations, actors, activities, and participation environments |
| **Aura** | Identity, reputation, and persistent actor history |
| **Flow** | Wallets, accounts, assets, and financial operations |
| **Synergy** | Scenarios, automation, directives, actions, and coordinated execution |

These services form a common foundation for specialized application domains.

The architecture supports a progression from simulated activity to live operation, allowing scenarios and participants to be developed, evaluated, and operated within a shared execution model.

### Participation Markets

TradeSphere's participation model brings humans and autonomous agents into environments governed by the same underlying architecture.

Organizations establish activities. Actors participate through directives and actions. Resources are committed, outcomes are evaluated, and incentives and consequences influence future participation.

The result is a framework for economic interaction that extends beyond conventional trading markets.

Applications can specialize in different kinds of participation without rebuilding the underlying execution infrastructure.

## Applications

### TradeSphere Academy — On-Chain Auctions

**Working application**

Academy is our dedicated on-chain auction application, integrating **Uniswap's Continuous Clearing Auction (CCA) protocol** with TradeSphere's scenario-driven execution capabilities.

The application includes wallet connectivity, competitive bidding, real-time auction visualization, clearing calculations, settlement, and claims.

Our execution framework also supports automated bidders, simulation, and replay for evaluating auction behavior and results.

Academy's first featured campaign, **Gridiron: The Reckoning**, begins with a Discovery and qualification auction on Ethereum Sepolia, with a conditional progression to mainnet.

### Gridiron — Competitive Participation

**Public preview**

Gridiron explores participation through leagues, teams, games, competition, and community-driven activities.

Its application experience is available in the public TradeSphere preview. Blockchain and smart contract integration remain part of the development roadmap.

### Whaler — Markets and Strategy

**Public preview**

Whaler focuses on market participation through ideas, missions, signals, collaborative strategies, and economic activities.

Its application experience is available in the public TradeSphere preview, with additional blockchain integration planned.

## Technology & Engineering

TradeSphere combines modern application development, blockchain infrastructure, and scenario-driven execution.

Our technology includes:

- **Microsoft .NET / Blazor** — Application framework and services
- **Fluent UI Blazor** — User interface components
- **Nethereum** — Ethereum integration for .NET
- **Uniswap CCA** — On-chain auction price discovery and settlement
- **Reown** — Wallet connectivity
- **Ethereum / EVM** — Smart contracts and blockchain execution
- **Microsoft Azure** — Hosting and infrastructure

The platform also includes our own execution architecture, domain models, services, and simulation capabilities.

We're interested in the engineering challenges that arise when distributed systems, economic incentives, automation, and autonomous participants share the same operating environment.

## Development Direction

TradeSphere is an evolving platform with a substantial existing codebase and several distinct development efforts.

Current and planned work includes:

- **Academy:** Continued auction development and integration with TradeSphere organizational activities.
- **Aura:** Completing the identity, reputation, and actor architecture.
- **Flow:** Developing wallet and financial infrastructure.
- **Synergy:** Expanding scenario execution, automation, simulation, and replay capabilities.
- **Gridiron & Whaler:** Extending the public previews with blockchain and smart contract integration.
- **TradeSphere Node / MCP:** Developing interfaces for external systems and autonomous agents, including future agent signaling and participation.

These efforts are at different stages of development. Features described as future work are not presented as currently available capabilities.
